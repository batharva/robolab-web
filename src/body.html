RoboLab Web

Interactive robotics analysis studio — Denavit–Hartenberg modelling, forward and inverse kinematics, Jacobians, trajectories, dynamics and workspace analysis. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Choose an example robot to begin:

An independent educational tool. It is not affiliated with, and does not copy, any commercial or academic robotics package. Example robots are simplified educational models, not manufacturer-accurate models.

Help and shortcuts

Mouse

Drag to orbit · right-drag or two-finger drag to pan · wheel or pinch to zoom · Shift+click on the viewport to place the IK target on the plane at the target's current z.

Graphs

Wheel zooms the time axis (Shift+wheel zooms the vertical axis), drag pans, double-click resets, hover shows values. Click a legend chip to hide a series.

Keyboard (when not typing in a field)

F fit view · W wireframe · G grid · A frames · C perspective/orthographic · T clear trail · Space play/pause · [ ] toggle side panels · 1–0 switch analysis mode · ? this help · Ctrl+S save.
